# Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.

Work-from-home days require approval from the employee's manager.

Employees must remain available during normal working hours while working remotely.


# Casual Leave Policy

Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year.

Casual leave should normally be requested at least 1 working day in advance.

In case of an emergency, employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.


# Reimbursement Policy

Approved business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

Approved reimbursements are normally processed within 10 working days.


# Health Insurance

Full-time employees are eligible for company health insurance after completing 30 days of employment.
